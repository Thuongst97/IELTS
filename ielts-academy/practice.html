9.3How to use the practice tests

Exam conditions

  • Use Exam mode in Listening: each part plays once with reading time.
  • Start the 60-minute timers in Reading and Writing and stop when they run out.
  • No dictionary, no pausing, phone away. Sit the first three papers back-to-back in a full test.

Review afterwards

  • Every Listening and Reading answer has an explanation. Read them all — including the ones you got right by guessing.
  • Listening transcripts highlight the answer locations after marking.
  • Log mistakes by type (spelling, distractor, NOT GIVEN…) in your mistake log.

Writing & Speaking scores

  • These are self-assessed against the Band 7 criteria. Be strict: compare with the model answers in the Writing and Speaking modules.
  • Each task includes a Band 7 plan to check your ideas and structure after you finish.
  • Record your speaking test and listen back before you score it.

Suggested schedule

  • Tests 1–3: single skills, learning the formats.
  • Tests 4–8: one full test per week (see the 12-week plan).
  • Tests 9–10: final mocks in the last two weeks, at the same time of day as your real test.

All tests are original practice material in the IELTS style, not official IELTS papers. Charts in Writing Task 1 use invented practice data. Band conversions are typical estimates.

8.2What Band 6.5–7 sounds like

These answers are written to show a strong Band 7 speaker, the realistic target for most learners, rather than a native speaker. Here is what to listen for in each criterion, and what a Band 8 answer would add.

CriterionWhat you'll hear in these modelsWhat Band 8 would add
Fluency & coherenceSpeaks at length without obvious effort. Ideas are linked with natural markers (That said, On top of that, Having said that, So how do I feel about it?). Self-correction sounds normal (Well, not while I'm riding…).Almost no hesitation for language; ideas develop fully and flexibly every time.
Lexical resourceTopic vocabulary plus some less common phrases and idioms used correctly (a game-changer, a love-hate relationship, pulled together). Vietnamese words are explained clearly.A wider range of precise, less common words, used skilfully with very few slips.
Grammatical range & accuracyA mix of simple and complex sentences: conditionals, relative clauses, passives, present perfect and past perfect. Most sentences are error-free.A wide range of structures used flexibly; nearly every sentence is error-free.
PronunciationThis is your part. Use the 🔊 buttons to hear the rhythm, then record yourself. Focus on final consonants, -s/-ed endings and word stress.Easy to understand all the time; the accent has very little effect.

The browser voice is only a guide to rhythm and stress; it isn't a native speaker. The band levels describe the language in the answers. Your band also depends on how you say them.

8.3Talking about Vietnam in English

The examiner may not know what bánh chưng or nồm is. Never stop at the Vietnamese word: say it, then explain it in a few simple words. These six patterns appear again and again in the models.

PatternExample from the models
Name + which is…We eat bánh chưng, which is a square cake made of sticky rice, mung beans and pork.
what we call…It's what we call a “tube house” in Vietnam. It's very narrow, but it goes a long way back.
The name literally means…The áo dài is our national costume. The name literally means “long shirt”.
North / southIn the south, they're called chả giò.
Purpose: the idea is to…People clean their houses from top to bottom, because the idea is to start the new year fresh.
Place + distanceHội An is a small town on the central coast, about forty-five minutes' drive from Đà Nẵng.

Mistakes Vietnamese speakers often make

Instead ofSayWhy
I very like phở.I really like phở. / I like phở very much.Very can't go before a verb.
In my country have many festivals.There are many festivals in my country.A direct translation of có.
Vietnam people are friendly.Vietnamese people are friendly.Use the adjective before a noun.
I live in tube house.I live in a tube house.Singular countable nouns need an article.
Two year ago I visit Hội An.Two years ago I visited Hội An.Say the endings clearly: /s/, /t/, /d/.
Open / close the air conditioner.Turn on / turn off the air conditioning.Open is a direct translation of mở.

8.4How to practise with a model answer

  1. Answer first2–3 min

    Tick “Hide the model answers until I've tried”. Read the question (or press 🔊 to hear it) and record your own answer. For Part 2, use the 1-minute plan and 2-minute talk timer.

  2. Listen and compare3 min

    Reveal the model and press ▶ Listen. Did you answer directly? Did you give a reason and an example? Did you keep going for the full two minutes in Part 2?

  3. Borrow three phrases3 min

    Choose three items from “Language to reuse” and write your own sentence with each one, about your life.

  4. Record again3 min

    Answer the same question again with your own story and the new phrases. Compare the two recordings: the second should be longer and smoother.

⚠️
Don't memorise these answers

Examiners notice memorised answers quickly, and they will change the question to test you. The people and stories in the models are invented. Use them to learn how good answers are built, then talk about your own life.

IELTS Overview: Structure, Scoring & Band 7 Strategy

Before you practise, understand the test you are preparing for: what each paper contains, how examiners score you, how your overall band is calculated — and what a 7.0 actually looks like in each skill.

⏱ 20 min read🎯 Foundation for every other module🧮 Includes a band calculator

1.1What is IELTS?

The International English Language Testing System measures how well you can use English to study, work and live where English is spoken. It is jointly owned by the British Council, IDP IELTS and Cambridge University Press & Assessment, and is accepted by thousands of universities, employers, professional bodies and immigration authorities worldwide.

IELTS Academic

For university study (undergraduate or postgraduate) and professional registration, e.g. doctors, nurses, engineers.

IELTS General Training

For migration (e.g. Australia, Canada, New Zealand, UK), work experience and secondary education.

IELTS for UKVI / Life Skills

Versions approved for UK visa applications. Same content as Academic/GT (Life Skills tests only speaking & listening).

DeliveryOn computer at a test centre, or on paper. The content and scoring are identical; Speaking is always face-to-face with an examiner (in some locations via video call).
ResultsComputer-delivered: usually within a few days. Paper-based: about 13 days. Each skill gets a band from 0 to 9, plus an overall band.
ValidityMost organisations accept results for 2 years.
One Skill RetakeIn many countries you can retake a single skill (e.g. only Writing) within 60 days of a computer-delivered test, instead of the whole test. Check whether your target organisation accepts it.
Typical 7.0 requirementsMany universities ask for overall 6.5–7.0 with no band below 6.0–6.5; competitive programmes, medicine and law often want 7.0–7.5. Always check the minimum per skill, not just the overall.

Policies (fees, result times, retake rules) change — confirm details on ielts.org or with your test centre before you book.

1.5The 9-band scale

Every result is reported on the same scale from 0 to 9, in half bands (e.g. 6.5, 7.0). The descriptions below summarise the official user levels.

BandLevelWhat it means
9ExpertFull operational command: appropriate, accurate and fluent, with complete understanding.
8Very goodFully operational command with only occasional unsystematic inaccuracies; may misunderstand in unfamiliar situations. Handles complex, detailed argument well.
7Good ✦ your targetOperational command, with occasional inaccuracies and misunderstandings in some situations. Generally handles complex language well and understands detailed reasoning.
6CompetentGenerally effective command despite some inaccuracies and misunderstandings. Can use fairly complex language, particularly in familiar situations.
5ModestPartial command; copes with overall meaning in most situations, but makes many mistakes. Basic communication in own field.
4LimitedBasic competence limited to familiar situations; frequent problems with understanding and expression.
3Extremely limitedConveys and understands only general meaning in very familiar situations.
2IntermittentGreat difficulty understanding spoken and written English.
1Non-userNo ability to use the language except a few isolated words.
0Did not attemptNo assessable information provided.

How the overall band is calculated

The overall band is the average of the four skills, rounded to the nearest half band. A fraction of .25 rounds up to the next half band and .75 rounds up to the next whole band:

1.6Scoring criteria by skill

🎧 Listening & 📖 Reading — objective marking

  • 1 mark per correct answer, 40 marks in total.
  • The raw score is converted to a band using a table (see below).
  • Spelling and grammar of answers must be correct; word limits must be respected.
  • No negative marking — never leave a blank.

✍️ Writing — 4 criteria × 25%

  • Task Achievement (T1) / Task Response (T2): answering all parts, overview / clear position, developed ideas.
  • Coherence & Cohesion: logical organisation, paragraphing, linking and referencing.
  • Lexical Resource: range, precision, collocation, spelling, word formation.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: variety of structures and proportion of error-free sentences.

Task 2 carries twice the weight of Task 1.

🎙️ Speaking — 4 criteria × 25%

  • Fluency & Coherence: speaking at length without effort, logical sequencing, discourse markers.
  • Lexical Resource: range, idiomatic language, paraphrase.
  • Grammatical Range & Accuracy: complex structures, error-free sentences.
  • Pronunciation: individual sounds, word and sentence stress, intonation, ease of understanding.

🧭 What this means for you

  • In Writing and Speaking, a weakness in one criterion pulls the whole band down — e.g. TR 7, CC 7, LR 7 but GRA 6 averages 6.75 for that task — not a 7.
  • Accuracy (grammar, spelling) affects three skills: Listening/Reading answers, Writing GRA and Speaking GRA.
  • Band 7 descriptors for each criterion are explained in the Writing and Speaking modules.

1.10What Band 7 really means

Band 7 is a “good user”: you communicate complex ideas effectively, with occasional errors that don't cause misunderstanding. Here is what that looks like in each skill.

Listening
30/40

Near-perfect in Parts 1–2; follows academic discussion and lectures; spelling accurate under pressure.

Reading
30/40

Finishes in 60 minutes; distinguishes FALSE from NOT GIVEN; identifies a writer's views reliably.

Writing
7.0

Clear position, developed ideas, logical paragraphs, precise collocations, frequent error-free complex sentences.

Speaking
7.0

Speaks at length without noticeable effort; uses some idiomatic language; mostly error-free; easy to understand.

1.11Your Band 7 strategy

Because the overall band is an average, you can reach 7.0 with different profiles. Most successful Vietnamese candidates use their receptive skills to balance Writing:

ProfileLRWSOverallComment
Balanced7.07.07.07.07.0Safest if your university needs “no band below 7”.
Receptive-strong7.57.56.56.57.0The most common route — but check minimum requirements.
Very receptive-strong8.07.56.06.57.0Risky: 6.0 in Writing is often below the minimum.
Just short7.07.06.56.56.75 → 7.0Exactly at the rounding edge — one half band less anywhere drops you to 6.5.
  1. Diagnose firstTake one full practice test (use this site's Listening and Reading tests and one essay) to find your current profile.
  2. Secure Listening & Reading at 7.5These improve fastest with technique and practice; they create a buffer for Writing.
  3. Fix accuracy in Writing & SpeakingMoving from 6.0 to 7.0 mostly means fewer errors and better-developed ideas, not harder vocabulary.
  4. Follow a planThe 12-week roadmap schedules all four skills and tracks your mock scores.

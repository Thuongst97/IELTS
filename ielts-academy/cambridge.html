10.1How the Cambridge companion works

©
Why the questions aren't on this site

Cambridge IELTS tests (passages, recordings, questions and answer keys) are copyrighted by Cambridge University Press & Assessment, so they can't be republished on a public website. This page holds no Cambridge content. You bring the book; the site does everything else. The answers and keys you type stay only in your browser.

1 · Do the test

Open your book and play the official audio (online resources). Type your answers into the 40-box answer sheet. The timer starts with your first answer.

2 · Enter the key

Copy the answer key from the back of the book once: box by box, or paste it all. Alternatives, optional words in brackets and either-order pairs are understood.

3 · Mark & review

Get your raw score and band, then go through every wrong answer: an automatic diagnosis (spelling, word limit, NOT GIVEN…), a fix, and a box for the evidence from the audioscript or passage.

4 · Track your weaknesses

Results build a profile across all your Cambridge tests: accuracy by question type and the reasons you lose marks, so you know what to practise next.

📌
Which books?

Cambridge IELTS 15–19 (20 tests) are the closest to today's exam, so start there. Keep the newest books (20–21) for your final mocks in the last 3–4 weeks. The older books (1–14) are still useful for extra Reading and Listening practice. Use the Academic or General Training edition that matches your test.

10.4My Cambridge results

Listening and Reading bands use typical conversion tables. Each Cambridge book prints its own table, which can differ by a mark. Writing and Speaking are your self-assessments. Send a complete test to the mock-test tracker to chart your progress.

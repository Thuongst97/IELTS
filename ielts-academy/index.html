What 7.0 takes

Your Band 7 targets

The overall band is the average of the four skills. These are the numbers to aim for in each paper.

🎧 Listening
30/40

Near-perfect Parts 1–2, accurate spelling, no distractor traps.
📖 Reading
30/40

Finish in 60 minutes; master TRUE/FALSE/NOT GIVEN and headings.
✍️ Writing
7.0

Clear position, developed ideas, frequent error-free complex sentences.
🎙️ Speaking
7.0

Speak at length without effort, natural collocations, clear final sounds.

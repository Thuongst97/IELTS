6.2How to study a model answer

  1. Plan before you read3–5 min

    Read the question and make your own plan: overview points for Task 1, or a position and two main ideas for Task 2. Better still, tick “Try it yourself first” and write a full answer against the timer.

  2. Compare the structure5 min

    Did the model group the data or arrange its arguments the way you did? Look closely at the overview (Task 1) and the thesis and topic sentences (Task 2).

  3. Steal the language, not the sentences5 min

    Copy 3–5 collocations or structures from “Language to reuse” into your notebook, with your own example sentence for each.

  4. Rewrite one paragraph10 min

    Close the model and rewrite your weakest paragraph using what you learned. Then check it against the Band 7+ frameworks.

⚠️
Don't memorise essays

Examiners are trained to recognise memorised answers, and memorised language is not credited. Real exam questions are always worded differently, so learn how the models are built, then practise writing your own answers.

All charts use invented practice data. The model answers are original and written for this site.

8.3Daily & weekly routine

Weekdays · 90 minutes

20 MINListeningDictation trainer (5 min) + one test section or podcast with transcript.
20 MINReadingOne timed passage, then justify every answer.
30 MINWriting or SpeakingAlternate days: one Task 1/2 or one essay plan + paragraph; or Part 2 + Part 3 recorded.
20 MINVocabulary & grammarFlashcard review (due cards) + one grammar section or quiz.

Weekend

SATURDAY · 3 HMock test + reviewListening, Reading, Writing back-to-back under exam conditions. Review mistakes the same day.
SUNDAY · 1 HSpeaking & planningFull speaking test recorded; log scores in the tracker; plan next week's focus.

8.6Trusted resources

ResourceUse it for
Cambridge IELTS Academic 15–20 (Cambridge University Press)Authentic past papers, the most accurate mock tests. Do at least 8 full tests, and mark them with the Cambridge answer sheets.
Official IELTS practice materials (ielts.org, British Council, IDP)Free sample tests, computer-delivered test familiarisation, sample Writing answers with examiner comments.
Public band descriptors (Writing & Speaking, on ielts.org)Read them once a week; they are your marking scheme.
BBC Learning English · 6 Minute EnglishShort listening with transcripts and vocabulary.
TED Talks, The Guardian Long Read, The ConversationPart 4-style lectures and Passage 3-style articles.
Cambridge Dictionary · YouGlish · OzdicPronunciation, real examples and collocations for every new word.
A teacher or study partnerFeedback on Writing and Speaking is the fastest way past 6.5. Even 4–5 marked essays make a difference.
